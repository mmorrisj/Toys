# Back to Evelyn

A game designed by Evie.

A toy is taken from the house where it belongs. It wakes up in a stranger's
bedroom, escapes down a laundry chute, gets scooped up by a bin lorry, and has
to cross a garbage dump and a whole city at night to get home.

## Playing it

Open `index.html` in a browser. That's the whole install.

**Act One, Beat One — the bedroom.** You wake up in a room that isn't yours,
with three toys who have been here far too long. The doorknob is much too high.

- Arrow keys or WASD — walk
- Space — talk to a toy, and ask them to follow
- X — let everyone go and start the stack again
- Q — squeak
- R — start over
- M — mute

The puzzle is the order they stack in. Everything you need is in what they say.

## The files

- `index.html` — the bedroom prototype
- `sketch-tiles.html` — an earlier throwaway sketch of the tile/push engine

## Editing it

Everything lives at the top of `index.html`: the room's furniture, the cast,
their dialogue, and the correct stacking order. No build step, no installing
anything — change a line, save, refresh the browser.
